About the Project

Boe eno moto ("Bororo world") is a platform dedicated to the preservation, documentation, and appreciation of the linguistic and cultural knowledge of the Bororo people.

This repository brings together a variety of linguistic and ethnographic resources, both historical and contemporary, with the goal of supporting teaching, research, and revitalization efforts for the Bororo language. The platform offers a set of tools aimed at linguistic and anthropological study.
