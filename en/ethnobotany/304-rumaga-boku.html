Ethnobotany

Rumaga (boku)

Jatobá-do-boku

Hymenaea stigonocarpa Mart. ex Hayne

Family
Fabaceae
Use
Boe ere kurudo tugeje enogwagere ji. Nowu i ure turugado boe ewai, ika, epače. Ere nowu i biri mugudo joru tada boe ečegurugudo epa, boe ekigori epa. Ere nowu a kowu joruto ere nowu tubegaguru epado ema. Ere nowu kodu, nowu i kuru tadu, epado boe emearudu ewaduwo pudui, tugu čowo. Ere nowu biri okodo tapira mokuro kuru kao tuiorubodo ema
Fruiting
Junho a outubro
Habitat
boku
