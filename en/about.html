Boe Eno Moto

Digital platform for documentation and revitalization of the Bororo language and culture

Mission

Boe Eno Moto (Bororo World) is an initiative of the Indigenous Languages Platform dedicated to the documentation, preservation, and revitalization of the Bororo people's language and culture.

Our goal is to make linguistic, ethnographic, and cultural materials available in accessible digital formats, contributing to the strengthening of Bororo identity and to academic research on Brazilian indigenous languages.

Team & Partners

This project is developed in collaboration with:

Unifacc — University center, institutional partner
Salesian Mission of Mato Grosso — Historic partnership with indigenous peoples of the region
Uppsala University — Support in computational linguistics and technology
